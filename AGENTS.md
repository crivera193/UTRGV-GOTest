## Live gameplay direction — October 22, 2026

- Owner requires live Supabase gameplay; do not introduce device-local combat or production demo fallbacks. Use a few clearly labeled placeholder questions until the owner supplies the bank.
- Standard live rule set defaults: four questions per attack, each with a 15-second server deadline. Points and rule sets are editable in Supabase; the app must read their catalog and settings dynamically, with no point-specific code or rebuild required. Existing attempts retain immutable rule/cooldown snapshots; moving or disabling a point ends its active attempts while preserving history. With the standard defaults, correct kicks deal 6.25% of maximum point health; misses, wrong answers and timeouts deal zero and advance automatically. An attempt deals at most 25%. A ten-minute per-account/per-point cooldown starts at entry, survives capture and app restarts, and does not reset on Close. Unanswered questions continue expiring while away. The final damaging attacker's permanent team wins ownership; restore full health on capture.
- Canonical permanent teams, random question/choice snapshots, adjudication, HP, capture and idempotent receipts belong to Supabase. Preserve active questions on close/reopen; never trust a client-provided answer result, team or damage value. See docs/flame-games.md for the live contract and verification.
